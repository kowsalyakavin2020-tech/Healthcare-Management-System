Before you arrive

A little prep,
a gentler visit

Bring these and your appointment runs smoother — and if it's your first time, we'll walk you in from the door.

  • Photo ID & insurance card

    One government ID and your policy number if you have one.

  • Your medication list

    Names and doses of anything you're taking, including over-the-counter.

  • Earlier reports or scans

    Not essential — we'll request them — but they help the first consult.

  • 15 minutes early

    Enough time to park, register and settle in without the rush.
